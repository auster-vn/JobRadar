JobRadar distributes a modified compact derivative, not the raw dataset. The
changes filter to disclosed salary rows within 1-200 million VND per month,
convert documented USD values at 25,000 VND/USD, normalize titles, experience
and locations, remove URL tracking parameters, and add provenance metadata.
The supplied per-record posting dates are retained without interpolation.
